Privacy notice

NSEYIN is a massage therapy practice based at Boundary, Ajegunle, Lagos, Nigeria. Privacy contact: utibedc@gmail.com. Business name: NSEYIN.

What we collect

  • Booking requests: name, email, phone number, preferred contact method, chosen service, session length, where you would like the session (workspace, home service or private suite), preferred date and time of day, and any optional note you add.
  • Contact messages: name, email and your message.
  • Technical data: to protect the forms from abuse we briefly process a salted, one-way hash of your IP address to limit repeated submissions. The raw address is not stored.

Please do not include medical or health details in forms. We do not ask for them online. If something is relevant to your safety, you can tell us in person, and we use it only to keep you safe during your session.

Why we use it

To respond to your request, arrange and manage appointments, keep a record of bookings and contact attempts, and run the business. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

Your information is stored in a database and sent as email through service providers acting on our behalf. The database is run by Supabase, emails are sent through Resend, and this website is hosted by Netlify. These providers may process data outside Nigeria.

How long we keep it

  • Contact messages, and booking requests that do not become a session: deleted after 30 days.
  • If you have a session: your name, contact details and booking history are kept for 12 months after the session, so we can recognise returning clients, handle deposits and refunds, and apply our cancellation terms. They are then deleted.
  • Deletion runs automatically every day. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, subject to legal obligations. Email us at utibedc@gmail.com.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. Visit counting with Google Analytics is optional: it runs only if the site owner has switched it on and you press Accept on the small banner. If you press Decline, or never see a banner, no analytics runs. Google may process this anonymous usage data outside Nigeria. Advertising and personalisation features are turned off. Booking and contact details are never sent to analytics. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie choices” in the footer.

Booking terms

Requests and confirmation. Submitting the online form is a request only. An appointment exists only once NSEYIN has contacted you and agreed a date and time with you.

Payment. By bank transfer or cash, after your appointment is confirmed: by transfer, or on site before the service starts. We do not take payment on this website.

Deposit. No deposit is needed for sessions at the workspace. For Home service, Private suites and custom-length sessions we ask for a deposit of 30% of the session price (of the agreed price, for a custom length), paid by bank transfer after we confirm, to hold your time. It counts toward your price, and the rest is paid on the day. If you have had two late cancellations or no-shows, we may also ask for a deposit on workspace bookings.

Where. Sessions take place at the NSEYIN workspace (Boundary, Ajegunle, Lagos), as a home service, or at a private suite. For home service and private suites, the client covers transportation, and we agree the transport cost when we confirm. Before a home or suite visit we confirm the address and who will be there, and we may decline or end a visit that does not feel safe or professional.

Length and price. Sessions are 30 minutes or 1 hour at the listed prices. A custom length of 15 minutes or more can be agreed, with the price agreed outside the listed prices.

Suitability. Massage is not suitable for everyone at every time. NSEYIN may ask about suitability, may suggest you see a doctor or physiotherapist first, and may decline or stop treatment where it would not be appropriate. Massage supports comfort, relaxation and mobility and is not a medical treatment.

Complaints. See Complaints below.

Cancellations and rescheduling

These terms are a starting point and may change. The terms that applied when you booked are the ones that apply to your booking.

Cancelling or rescheduling

  • At least 12 hours before your appointment: you can reschedule for free, or cancel. If you paid a deposit or the full price, we refund it by bank transfer (usually within 3 working days) or move it to your new time, whichever you prefer.
  • Less than 12 hours before, or if you do not come: a fee of 30% of the session price applies. This is your deposit, if you paid one. If you paid more than that, we refund or move the rest. For workspace bookings with no deposit, we do not charge the first time, and we may ask for a deposit on your next booking.
  • Two late cancellations or no-shows: we may ask for a deposit on future workspace bookings.

If you are running late

Please message us. We hold your time for 15 minutes. If you arrive within 15 minutes, we shorten your session so we finish on time, or you can reschedule or cancel. After 15 minutes without a message, it counts as a no-show.

If we need to cancel or move your appointment

You get a full refund, or priority rebooking if you prefer, and no fee.

Complaints

We want to put things right. Tell us what happened by email (utibedc@gmail.com) or by WhatsApp message (+234 802 869 9824, messages only, not a calling number). We reply within 24 hours and aim to give you a full answer within 7 days. Concerns about behaviour or boundaries are taken seriously, and you can ask us to keep them private.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC). For a concern about how your personal data is handled, you can contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Consent and conduct

Consent is informed, voluntary, specific and ongoing. You may change pressure, decline a technique, pause or stop at any time. Permission for one area or technique never implies permission for another. NSEYIN provides professional therapeutic massage only. Sexual or inappropriate requests or behaviour will end the session, and no refund is given. See How we work.
