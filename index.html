How we work

02 · CONSENT

Respect, privacy and clear consent are part of every session, not a footnote. These principles apply to every client.

  • You are treated with dignity and respect at all times.
  • Your personal and professional boundaries are respected.
  • You will know the intended treatment areas before the session begins.
  • Consent is informed, voluntary and specific to the treatment.
  • You can ask questions, decline a technique, change the pressure, or ask us to pause or stop, at any time.
  • Permission for one area or technique never implies permission for another. A new area needs a separate conversation and your consent first.
  • Your privacy, comfort and draping are respected throughout.
  • You can withdraw consent at any point, with no pressure and no need to explain.

Important: agreeing to this policy when you request a booking does not give blanket permission for physical contact. Consent is a conversation at the start of the session and an ongoing choice throughout it.

What we offer: professional therapeutic massage only. Nothing we offer is sexual in nature, and inappropriate requests or behaviour will end the session.

Should I tell you about injuries or health conditions?

Please do not put medical details in the online forms. If something is relevant, we will discuss it with you privately before the session. If you have an acute injury, unexplained swelling or another concerning symptom, please see a doctor or physiotherapist first.

What happens at the start of a session?

We talk about what you want, the areas we would work on and the pressure you prefer. You will know the intended treatment areas before we begin.

Will deep tissue massage hurt?

Deep Tissue pressure can be firm and sometimes intense, but it should stay within what you can comfortably tolerate. Always tell us if it is too much.

What does consent mean here?

Consent is informed, voluntary and specific. Agreeing to one area or technique does not imply agreement to another, and any new area needs a separate conversation. You can withdraw consent at any point. See How we work.

Does ticking the boundaries box when booking mean I have agreed to everything?

No. It only shows you have read the policy. Consent is discussed at the start of the session and continues throughout.

What about privacy and draping?

Your privacy and comfort are respected throughout, including how you are draped. Tell us if you would like anything done differently.
